COMMERCIAL LOAN AGREEMENT

SECTION 1: PARTIES
This Commercial Loan Agreement is entered into as of July 15, 2026, by and between Apex Ventures Inc. ("Borrower") and Horizon Commercial Bank ("Lender").

SECTION 2: LOAN VALUE AND INTEREST RATE
2.1 Principal Amount: The Lender agrees to provide a commercial term loan in the amount of $3,500,000 (Three Million Five Hundred Thousand Dollars).
2.2 Interest Rate: The loan shall bear interest at an annual rate equal to SOFR plus 3.00%.

SECTION 3: COVENANTS AND FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE
3.1 Financial Ratio: The Borrower shall maintain a Debt-Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) of not less than 1.35x at the end of each fiscal quarter.
3.2 Reporting: Borrower shall provide audited financial statements within 90 days of fiscal year-end.

SECTION 4: GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES
4.1 Jurisdiction: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware.
